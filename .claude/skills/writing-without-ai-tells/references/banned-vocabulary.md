# Banned vocabulary

Merged from anti-ai-slop-writing and humanizer, deduplicated. These appear far
more often in post-2023 text than before it, and they cluster: finding one
usually means finding three.

Replace with a concrete noun, a real number, or a restructured sentence. If no
concrete replacement exists, the sentence is probably not saying anything.

## Words

align with, aforementioned, boasts, bolster, bolstered, commence, comprehensive
(describing your own output), crucial, cutting-edge, delve, delves, delving,
elevate, embark, empower, empowering, encompass, encompassing, endeavor,
endeavour, enduring, enhance, enhancing, epic (non-literal), facilitate, foster,
fostering, game-changer, game-changing, garner, garnered, groundbreaking,
harnessing, highlight (as verb), highlighting, interplay, intricacies, intricate,
key (as adjective), landscape (figurative), leverage (as verb), meticulous,
meticulously, multifaceted, navigating (figurative), nuanced (as filler),
paramount, pivotal, profound, remarkable, revolutionise, revolutionize, robust
(outside engineering), seamless, seamlessly, showcase, showcasing, spearheading,
streamline, stunning, supercharge, synergies, synergy, tapestry, testament,
transformative, underscore, underscores, unprecedented, utilize (say "use"),
valuable, vibrant

## Phrases

at its core, at the end of the day, bridge the gap, buckle up, circle back,
firstly/secondly/thirdly, from X to Y (when not a real scale), here's the deal,
here's the thing, in a nutshell, in conclusion, in essence, in the realm of,
in today's [adjective] [noun], it goes without saying, it's important to note,
it's not just about X, it's Y, it's worth noting, let's break this down,
let's delve into, let's dive in, move the needle, moving forward, not just X
but Y, pain points, please don't hesitate to reach out, rest assured,
take it to the next level, the bottom line is, the deeper issue, the heart of
the matter, the real question is, this is where X comes in, thought leadership,
touch base, unlock the power of, value proposition (casual contexts),
what really matters, whether you're a X or a Y, without further ado

## Openers

Absolutely, Additionally, Certainly, Furthermore, Indeed, Importantly,
Interestingly, Moreover, Notably, Overall, Sure, "Great question", "That's a
great point", "I'd be happy to", "However, it's important to"

## Copula substitutes

Watch these separately. They are how models avoid writing "is" or "has":

boasts, features a, marks a, offers a, represents a, serves as, stands as
