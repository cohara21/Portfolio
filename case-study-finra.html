FINRA x SCADpro

Industry Collaboration

YearWinter 2026
Duration10 weeks

Overview

During this 10 week SCADpro collaboration, our team worked with FINRA to research and develop concepts for redesigning their logged-in systems. We focused on creating a cohesive, role-based digital experience that unifies separate platforms into intuitive workflows. Our goal was to simplify how Broker Dealer Staff, Investors, and regulators access and navigate these complex tools.

My Role

I focused on the research and prototyping phases to help our team understand how other financial institutions handle role-based dashboards. This research gave us a great starting point for our own design decisions. I then worked on building the high-fidelity prototype to show how our unified workflows would actually function in a real-world setting.
