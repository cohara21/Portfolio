Resume

Bringing competitive discipline to complex UX challenges.

I am a product designer with a BFA in UX Design from The Savannah College of Art and Design. Growing up in Boise, Idaho, I spent my life loving the outdoors, which gave me a natural appreciation for adventure, exploration, and figuring out how things connect. I bring that same curiosity to the digital world, combining hands-on research and prototyping to untangle complicated spaces.

My background as a college tennis player directly shapes my daily design practice. It has taught me to be disciplined, collaborative, and incredibly focused under pressure. Whether I am writing code or mapping out an interface, my goal is always to create clean, high-performance experiences that let people move forward with total confidence.
